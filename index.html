    Stroke order

    笔顺
    One entry per line. Each line can be:
    • Characters: 你好 or several words separated by spaces 谢谢 老师
    • Characters plus your own meaning: 你好 hello there (pinyin right after the characters is detected and used to pick the reading)
    • Pinyin to search: nǐ hǎo, ni3 hao3 or nihao, then pick the word
    Or press From a photo to read characters off a picture of a vocab list or textbook page.
